$$$$$ Requirements $$$$$
========================
1. Create Accounts : Users can create and accounts and log into the website.
2. Contact details : user can store their name and contact details.
3. Addresses : Users can add one or more addresses to their account abd can set a default shipping address.
4.Payment Methods : No Nessacery
	-> users can add one or more payment methods to their account and can set a default payment method.
5. Products : product details
6.Categories : Each product belongs in a category and cateogries can belong to other categories.
7. Number in Stock : The Website should keep[ track of the numbers of each product that is in stock.
8. Shopping Cart : Visitors can add one or more products to their shopping cart as part of their shopping experience. Shopping cart are not saved in the database unless they are logged in.
9. Payment details for Order : A user needs to provide their payment details and address details as part of placing an order.
10. Shipping method : No required in my current project, going further we will use this.
11. Order Status : The order and shipping process follows severals stages once the order is placed, such as processing, delivery in progress and delivered.
